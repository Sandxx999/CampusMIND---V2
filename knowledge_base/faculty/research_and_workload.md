## Overview
Policies on seed grant schemes, mandatory teaching workloads, and intellectual property/patents for full-time faculty.

## Details
- **Teaching Workload**:
  - Full Professors: Minimum 8 contact hours/week + [Ph.D.](../courses/academic_programs.md) supervision.
  - Associate Professors: Minimum 12 contact hours/week + active research.
  - Assistant Professors: Minimum 16 contact hours/week + lab supervision.
- **Research Seed Grants**: Up to INR 3,00,000 for approved proposals. Awardees must publish at least 2 papers in Q1/Q2 journals within 18 months.
- **Consultancy**: Revenue shared 70:30 (70% faculty, 30% IFHE).
- **Conferences & IP**: 100% financial assistance for one premier international conference per year. IP and patent filing costs are fully sponsored by IFHE.
