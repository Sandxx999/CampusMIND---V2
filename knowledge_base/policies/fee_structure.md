## Overview
Details the semester tuition fee structure for various programs, deadlines for payment, penalties for late payment, and available merit scholarships.

## Details
- **Fee Examples**: [B.Tech](../courses/academic_programs.md) (INR 1,25,000/sem), [BBA](../courses/academic_programs.md) (INR 95,000/sem), [MBA](../courses/academic_programs.md) (INR 2,25,000/sem).
- **Deadlines**: Fall (July 15), Spring (December 15). Grace periods of 10 days apply.
- **Late Fees**: 
  - 1-10 days post grace period: INR 100/day.
  - 11-25 days post grace period: INR 250/day.
  - >25 days: Suspension of registration and portal access.
- **Merit Scholarships**: 50% waiver for Top 5% ([CGPA](grading_system.md) >= 9.0), 25% waiver for Next 5% ([CGPA](grading_system.md) 8.5-8.99).
- **[Hostel Fees](hostel_regulations.md)**: AC Double (INR 65,000/sem), Non-AC Double (INR 45,000/sem), [Mess Advance](hostel_regulations.md) (INR 35,000/sem).
