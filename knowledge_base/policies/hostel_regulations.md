## Overview
Regulations regarding hostel entry curfews, leave procedures, and dining hall rules, along with strict safety and anti-ragging policies.

## Details
- **Curfews**: In-Gate Curfew is 8:30 PM (weekdays) and 9:00 PM (weekends). Block Curfew is 9:00 PM – 9:45 PM.
- **Out-Pass**: Local Out-Pass available for 6:00 AM – 8:30 PM. Outstation leave requires 24h advance approval from Chief Warden and parent confirmation.
- **Dining Hall**: Four meals served daily. Excessive food wastage leads to a warning.
- **Zero Tolerance Policies**:
  - Ragging leads to immediate suspension/expulsion.
  - Possession of alcohol, tobacco, or illicit substances leads to expulsion and legal prosecution.
- **Visitors**: Permitted only at Central Visitors Lounge between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM.
