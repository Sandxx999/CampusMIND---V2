## Overview
Exams are conducted in Spring (April-June) and Fall (November-December) semesters. Students must carry valid physical hall tickets to enter exam halls.

## Details
- **Timings**: Morning Session (Slot A) at 09:30 AM – 12:30 PM, Afternoon Session (Slot B) at 02:00 PM – 05:00 PM.
- **Hall Tickets**: Generated 10 days prior to exams on the ERP Portal.
- **Eligibility**: [Minimum 75% attendance](attendance_policy.md), [zero pending fee dues](fee_structure.md), and no active disciplinary issues.
- **Exam Hall Entry**: Digital copies on mobile phones are strictly prohibited. A physical printout and physical [RFID student ID](student_documents.md) are required.
- **Special Accommodations**: PwD candidates can request scribes/extra time (20 minutes per hour) by registering medical certificates 10 days before exams.
