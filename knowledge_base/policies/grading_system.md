## Overview
Grading operates on a 10-point Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) scale. Evaluation is split into Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) and [End-Semester Examination (ESE)](exam_schedules.md).

## Details
- **Evaluation Weightage**: CIE accounts for 40%, ESE accounts for 60%.
- **Grade Scale**: O (>=90%, 10 pts), A+ (80-89%, 9 pts), A (70-79%, 8 pts), B+ (60-69%, 7 pts), B (55-59%, 6 pts), C (50-54%, 5 pts), P (40-49%, 4 pts), F (<40%, 0 pts).
- **Formulas**: 
  - SGPA = Sum(Course Credit Units x Grade Points Earned) / Total Semester Credit Units Registered.
  - CGPA = Aggregate Grade Points Earned / Cumulative Total Credits Completed.
  - Equivalent Percentage (%) = (CGPA - 0.5) x 10.
- **Transcripts**: Official transcripts can be ordered for a processing fee of INR 1,000 (INR 300 per additional copy).
- **Re-evaluation**: Re-counting (INR 500) and Re-evaluation (INR 1,500) options are available within 7 days of result publication.
