## Overview
Policies for checking out library materials, overdue fines, and reference material usage.

## Details
- **Borrowing Limits**:
  - [Undergraduate students](../courses/academic_programs.md): up to 4 books for 14 days.
  - [Postgraduate students](../courses/academic_programs.md) & [faculty](../faculty/research_and_workload.md): up to 10 books for 30 days.
- **Overdue Fines**: $1.00 per day per standard book.
- **Reference Materials**: Reference books and rare journals cannot be checked out and must be used inside the main reading room.
