## Overview
Students must maintain a minimum attendance percentage to be eligible for [End-Semester and Mid-Term Examinations](exam_schedules.md).

## Details
- **Minimum Requirement**: Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered course to be eligible to sit for [End-Semester and final examinations](exam_schedules.md).
- **Condonation Policy**: Attendance between 65% and 74% may be condoned by the Dean on valid medical grounds upon payment of the condonation fee and submission of approved medical documentation within 3 days of return.
