## Overview
Procedures for requesting official university documents, degree attestation, and replacing lost RFID smart ID cards.

## Details
- **Bonafide Certificates**: Can be requested for Educational Bank Loans, Passports, and Travel Concessions. Usually processed in 24-48 hours. (Free for first copy, INR 100 for duplicate).
- **Transfer & Migration Certificates**: Processed in 3 business days after No-Dues clearance. Fee: INR 300.
- **RFID ID Card Replacement**: Replacement fee is INR 500. A temporary gate pass is issued immediately while the new card is dispatched within 24 hours.
- **WES & Third-Party Verification**: Direct digital [transcript](grading_system.md) dispatch available for WES. Employer background verification costs INR 1,000 and takes 3-5 business days.
