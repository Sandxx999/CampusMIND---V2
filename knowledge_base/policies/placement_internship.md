## Overview
Guidelines related to the Career Development Center (CDC), placement drives, dream offers, and summer/winter internships.

## Details
- **Registration**: Final year students must register by August 1 (Fee: INR 1,500).
- **Eligibility**: Minimum [CGPA](grading_system.md) 6.0. Tier-1 Dream Companies require [CGPA](grading_system.md) >= 7.5 and 0 backlogs. Tier-2 Companies allow max 1 active backlog.
- **One-Offer Policy**: A student is de-registered from subsequent drives upon receiving an offer.
- **Dream Offer Exception**: Students with an offer < INR 8.0 LPA can apply for ONE Dream Tier Company offering >= INR 12.0 LPA.
- **Internships**: Mandatory 8-12 week summer internship. Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs) are binding.
